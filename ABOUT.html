ABOUT ARTICLES

Hey there 🤍 Welcome to UAU — a space where uniqueness is celebrated. My name is Yashika Gautam, and I’ve always found makeup fascinating — not because it can change us, but because it allows us to express ourselves. I created UAU for beauty lovers who are just starting out, but feel conflicted about it. For women who feel like they don’t quite fit in — or who’ve been made to feel like their appearance is either too much or not enough. Here, imperfection isn’t something to hide. It’s often the very thing that makes you stand out. We’re constantly shown a version of beauty that looks the same — perfect skin, perfect lips, perfect features. But if we all looked like that, beauty would lose its meaning. Individuality would disappear. And honestly? Everything would be a little boring. That’s why I believe there are no rules to makeup. What looks beautiful on one face may not work the same way on another — and that doesn’t mean there’s anything wrong with you. It just means you’re different. And that’s allowed. On UAU, I’ll be sharing my personal makeup tips and tricks for different facial features, skin types, undertones, and moods — along with product reviews and skincare thoughts. Not from a place of perfection, but from curiosity, honesty, and experience. And always remember: Makeup is a form of self-expression — not a shield to disguise yourself. It’s art. Play with it. Experiment. Make your own rules. Be Unapologetically Unruly.
