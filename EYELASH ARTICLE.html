How to Get False-Lash Results Using Mascara

By Yashika Gautam

Long luscious eyelashes can elevate your entire eye look. It adds drama and that extra oomph to your eyes. However, many of us don’t like how false lashes feel on our eyes. Sometimes it feels like it weighs our eyes down and makes them look heavy. So here this article will show you a mascara technique that gives false-lash effect without actually wearing falsies.

Step-1 Start with clean lashes

One of the very first steps is to clean the lash before using any product on them. Make sure they are clean and makeup free. You don’t want to run your mascara wand through lashes covered with old, dried-up mascara. It could potentially damage your lashes. You can prep your lashes by cleaning them with a gentler cleanser or using a cotton pad soaked in makeup remover.

Step-2 Curl your lashes

Now this step is one of the most important steps. Using a lash curler helps to give your lashes a nice lift. Using mascara on its own won’t make your lashes look that feathery or long. Heating up your lash curler before using it helps hold the curl for a long time. Heat up the lash curler using a hair dryer, for a few seconds. Make sure it’s not too hot to touch. Then carefully place the curler on your lashes and give a good curl. Clamp the curler for a few times until lashes look nice and curled

Step-3 Apply mascara

The technique of applying mascara is what’s going to make a big difference. Use a mascara with a large wand. Take out the wand from the tube at an angle so that the excess product gets wiped off. To give your lashes extra volume, first apply mascara to the back of your lashes, this step may sound a little strange but it makes a huge difference. Now while its still wet quickly apply mascara to only the ends of the lashes while holding the wand in a vertical position.

Step-4 Separate the lashes out

out Using a spoolie or a lash comb separate your lashes out by brushing it through a few times. This avoids the lashes looking clumpy giving them a wispy look.

Step-5 Apply a second coat of mascara

The next step is to apply a second coat of mascara to the lashes. Re dip the wand in mascara tube again and wipe off the excess. This time you are supposed to apply it all the way from the roots to ends. Wiggle the wand in a zig -zag while brushing it through and use a lash comb once again.

Step-6 Use the lash curler again

Curl your lashes again but this time without heating the curler. You have to be really careful this time. Make sure you’re gentle while clamping the curler, so you don’t pull your lashes out.

Step-7 Apply eyeliner to your upper waterline

To give that lash-band effect to eyes apply black pencil to your upper waterline. It will take your look to another level

Step-8 Apply mascara to bottom lashes (optional)

You can apply mascara to bottom lashes to even out the look and make your eyes appear defined.
